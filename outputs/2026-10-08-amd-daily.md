## Stance: Hold

AMD remains a core holding because of its broad AI data-center exposure, deep partnerships with Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta, and a compelling strategy expanding beyond chips into rack-scale AI systems and software. These drivers underpin bullish analyst earnings forecasts suggesting that AMD can capitalize on the AI growth cycle despite recent calls to slow AI development.
