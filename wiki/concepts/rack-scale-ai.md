# Rack-scale AI

## Thesis

AMD is expanding from selling chips into rack-scale AI systems and software, aimed at agentic workloads and a large server-CPU market through 2030. That is a positioning claim versus Nvidia's GPU-centric stack, not a daily tape fact.
