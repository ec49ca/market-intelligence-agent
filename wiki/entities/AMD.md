# Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD)

## Thesis

AMD is a semiconductor company competing for AI data-center spend with GPUs, CPUs, and a push into rack-scale systems and software, not only discrete chips. Anchor customers named in coverage include Anthropic, OpenAI, and Meta. Valuation stays very high relative to the tape, so the thesis is that those AI relationships and the rack-scale shift can grow into the multiple — not that the stock is cheap.

> 2026-09-14: The session selloff on AI-development slowdown calls undercuts any claim that AMD's AI momentum is insulated from sector caution.
