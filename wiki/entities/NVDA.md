# NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA)

## Thesis

NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) continues to solidify its role as the premier AI-focused semiconductor company with a market capitalization around $5.5 trillion as of October 2026. Its core strength lies in providing leading-edge AI GPUs that fuel the unfolding AI infrastructure boom, underscored by a large partnership with Pinterest deploying 14,000 GPUs to power AI shopping and search services. Nvidia’s strategic equity stake in AI infrastructure partners such as CoreWeave (11.5%) and ecosystem validations like Firmus’s multi-year OpenAI agreement in Malaysia underpin its dominant position. Despite intense competition and supply constraints, NVIDIA manages a calm volatility environment and maintains strong positive price momentum supported by persistent AI demand.

The economics of AI infrastructure remain complex, with questions raised by major customers like Microsoft regarding the true utility (‘useful yield’) of investments in AI hardware and energy efficiency. Meanwhile, competitive moves by players like AMD confirm a growing market stretching beyond Nvidia’s reach.
